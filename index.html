About me

My focus is on improving user journeys and business metrics through research and hypothesis-driven design.
I love clean & clear interfaces that are accessible by default and meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

Design systems are a core part of that work, built and evolved from the ground up to keep products consistent as they scale.
From problem discovery to delivery, I work directly with clients and stakeholders — presenting ideas, aligning on goals and justifying design decisions.
